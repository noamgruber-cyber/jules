Glossary of Terms

A quick reference for common stock market and technical analysis terminology.

Bear Market
A market condition in which the prices of securities are falling or are expected to fall, typically by 20% or more from recent highs.
Bull Market
A financial market of a group of securities in which prices are rising or are expected to rise.
Breakout
When a price moves above a resistance area, or moves below a support area.
Consolidation
A period of range-bound activity after a price movement; a pause before the next major price movement.
Divergence
When the price of an asset is moving in the opposite direction of a technical indicator, such as an oscillator.
Moving Average
An indicator that smooths out price data by creating a constantly updated average price over a specific period.
Pullback
A temporary pause or dip in an asset's overall trend. The term is sometimes used interchangeably with 'retracement' or 'consolidation'.
Resistance
A price level where a rising price tends to stop and reverse direction, acting as a "ceiling".
Support
A price level where a falling price tends to stop and reverse direction, acting as a "floor".
Trendline
A line drawn over pivot highs or under pivot lows to show the prevailing direction of price. Trendlines are a visual representation of support and resistance in any time frame.
