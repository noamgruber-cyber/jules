Institutional-Grade Analysis

Moving beyond basic trendlines: Order Flow, Derivatives, and Quantitative Backtesting.

1. Order Flow & Liquidity

Traditional technical analysis often lags behind real-time market mechanics. Order flow tools provide an "x-ray" into the market, showing what is happening inside the candles right now.

Footprint Charts

Instead of a solid candlestick, a footprint chart displays the actual traded volume at every specific price level inside that candle. It helps identify exactly where aggressive buyers or sellers stepped in.

Depth of Market (DOM) Heatmaps

Visualizes the limit order book, showing where massive "Buy Walls" and "Sell Walls" are resting. These areas act as magnetic liquidity pools that often draw price toward them before causing sharp reversals.

2. Derivative Market Mechanics

In modern markets, the derivatives tail often wags the equity dog. Understanding options flow is critical for anticipating major equity moves.

Gamma Exposure (GEX)

Identifies crucial levels where Market Makers (dealers) are forced to buy or sell the underlying stock to hedge their options exposure. High GEX levels act as massive support/resistance "flip zones."

Options Flow Aggregator

Tracking massive, unusual options trades (sweeps and blocks). When institutional "whales" quietly position themselves via options ahead of a major catalyst, flow tools detect the footprints.

3. Monte Carlo Strategy Simulator

A "Monte Carlo" simulation runs thousands of random scenarios based on your strategy's metrics to determine the probability of "Account Ruin" versus long-term success. It answers the question: "If I trade this system 100 times, what happens?"
