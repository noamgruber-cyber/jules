The Mathematics of Trading

Risk management is the most critical component of a trader's longevity. While charting helps identify opportunities, risk management ensures you survive long enough to capitalize on them.

Risk/Reward Ratio

The Risk/Reward ratio compares the potential profit of a trade to its potential loss. A minimum ratio of 1:2 means you aim to make $2 for every $1 risked. This ensures that even if you win less than 50% of the time, you can still be profitable.

Expectancy Model

Expectancy tells you what your average return per trade will be over the long term. A positive expectancy means your system is profitable.

E = (Win% × Avg Win) - (Loss% × Avg Loss)
